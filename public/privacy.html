Privacy policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026

Draft template, pending review. Not legal advice.

Who we are

ClockWise (clockwiseapp.dev) is a free world-clock tool run by OverStacked, the data controller for this site. Contact: hello@clockwiseapp.dev.

What we collect

ClockWise has no accounts, sign-up, or forms. The zones and times you pick are handled in your browser and are not sent to us.

Analytics, only with your consent. If you click Accept in the cookie banner, we load Google Analytics 4 to understand how many people use the site and how. Google Analytics then sets cookies (_ga, _ga_*) and collects usage data such as pages viewed, approximate location (country/city derived from your IP address, which Google Analytics 4 does not log or store), device, browser, referring site, and interactions. Google Signals and ad personalisation are turned off, and advertising consent signals stay denied. If you click Decline, or make no choice, Google Analytics is never loaded and no request is made to Google.

Legal basis: your consent (GDPR / UK GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)), and consent for cookies under ePrivacy / PECR rules.

Google as processor and international transfers

Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC processes analytics data on our behalf. Data may be transferred to and processed in the United States. Google relies on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (and its UK extension) and on Standard Contractual Clauses for these transfers. Analytics data is kept for the retention period set in our Google Analytics settings (at most 14 months). See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses data from sites that use its services.

Stored in your browser

ClockWise saves two small items in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device:

  • clockwise.analyticsConsent: your analytics choice (accepted or declined).
  • A display preference, such as 12-hour or 24-hour time.

These are needed to remember your choices and are not used for tracking. You can clear them through your browser settings at any time.

No other third parties

Fonts and map data are served from our own site, not from third-party CDNs. The site is hosted on Vercel, which processes standard server logs (such as IP addresses) to deliver and secure the site.

Changing or withdrawing consent

Use Cookie settings in the footer of ClockWise at any time. If you withdraw consent, we stop loading Google Analytics, try to delete its cookies, and reload the page. You can also delete cookies through your browser.

Your rights

If you are in the EU, UK, or another place with similar laws, you have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict our processing of your personal data, to object, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing. Because we don't hold accounts, we usually cannot identify you from analytics data; Google Analytics data is deleted when its retention period ends. To make a request, contact hello@clockwiseapp.dev. You can also complain to your local data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO).

Children

ClockWise is not directed at children under 16.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change and revise the effective date above.
